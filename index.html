Wobble Wheels

Draw your wheels. Beat your friends.

A racing game where you draw your own wheels, then redraw them mid-race when the road changes. Spikes bite on ice, paddles get you through floods, small wheels fit low tunnels. Race friends live, chase world-record flags and climb ranked seasons.

AnyLap

A lap timer for RC cars that runs on your phone's camera.

Point your phone at the finish line and AnyLap counts laps and times them as the cars cross it. No transponders, no extra hardware.

One tap. Everyone knows.

About VICCOM

VICCOM started in 2026 as a one-person studio in Sweden, run by Victor Immonen. We make games for mobile and desktop, and apps for things we were missing ourselves. Clear design, things worth coming back to, and no pay to win.

We share the work along the way: sketches, prototypes and ideas that didn't make it. Most of it shows up on Instagram and TikTok first.
