Who is responsible

VICCOM is a sole proprietorship run by Victor Immonen in Sweden, and is the data controller for everything described here. Contact us at info@viccom.se.

We're a small studio and only collect what a feature actually needs. We never sell personal data.

This website

viccom.se uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. Fonts are hosted on our own site, so visiting it sends nothing to third parties.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service running and secure. The browser version of Wobble Wheels at viccom.se/play is covered under Wobble Wheels below.

When you email us

If you write to one of our addresses, we keep your name, email address and message so we can answer and help you. Email runs on Google Workspace.

  • Support and general questions are kept for up to 24 months after our last contact. Legal basis: legitimate interest, or contract if it concerns something you bought.
  • Orders and receipts are kept for seven years, because Swedish bookkeeping law requires it.

Our games and apps

Store purchases and downloads through Google Play, the App Store, Steam or itch.io are handled by the store, which is responsible for its own data, such as payment details. We don't receive your payment details.

None of our products show ads or use third-party analytics today. If that changes, we update this policy first.

Wobble Wheels

When you start the game, you get a guest account automatically, so your progress and online races work without signing up. We store a player ID, the display name you choose, your progress, garage and settings, and your race results, records and leaderboard placings. Your display name and results are visible to other players on leaderboards and in live races. You can link the guest account to Google to keep it across devices.

Data is stored with Supabase in Ireland (EU). The browser version also keeps some settings in your browser's local storage. Legal basis: contract, to deliver the game and its online features.

Wyrmwind

Wyrmwind is a prototype and doesn't collect any personal data. We'll update this section before it does.

AnyLap

AnyLap uses your phone's camera to detect cars crossing the finish line. The camera image is analysed on your device and isn't uploaded or stored by us. If you sign in, we store your account, sessions and lap times so you can compare them later.

Data is stored with Supabase in Stockholm (EU). AnyLap and Psst share one VICCOM account. Legal basis: contract.

Psst

Psst needs an account to know which groups you belong to. We store your account, your display name, the groups you're in and the alerts sent in them. To deliver alerts we store a push token for your device, and the alert is delivered through Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (and Apple Push Notification service on iPhone).

Data is stored with Supabase in Stockholm (EU). Psst and AnyLap share one VICCOM account. Legal basis: contract.

Accounts and sign-in

Some products let you sign in with Google or Apple. We then receive a user ID and, if you allow it, your email address and name. If you hide your email when signing in with Apple, we only get Apple's forwarding address. We never receive your password.

You can delete your account at any time, in the app or by email. See Delete account for how, and what gets deleted.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you're a parent and think your child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase for databases and accounts, in Ireland and Stockholm (EU).
  • Google Workspace for email.
  • GitHub Pages for hosting this website.
  • Expo and Google Firebase for push notifications in Psst.
  • Google and Apple for sign-in, if you choose to use them.

Some are US companies. Transfers outside the EU/EEA rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • know what data we hold about you and get a copy,
  • have incorrect data corrected,
  • have your data deleted, unless the law requires us to keep it,
  • restrict or object to how we use it,
  • get data you gave us in a machine-readable format.

To delete an account, follow Delete account. For anything else, email info@viccom.se and we'll answer within a month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, imy.se.

Changes

We update this policy when we change how we handle data, for example when a product launches or gets a new feature. The date at the top shows the latest change.
