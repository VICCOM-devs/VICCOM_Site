Delete account

How to delete your account in a VICCOM game or app, and what happens to your data.

Contents

  1. Delete in the app
  2. Delete by email
  3. What gets deleted
  4. What we keep
  5. Delete only some data

Developer: VICCOM. Applies to Wobble Wheels and Psst. Uninstalling an app does not delete your account. AnyLap has no account: everything it saves is on your phone, and you delete it by removing racers and sessions in the app or by uninstalling it.

Delete in the app

  1. Open the game or app.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

The account is deleted straight away and you're signed out.

Delete by email

If you no longer have the app, or can't get in, we can delete the account for you.

  1. Email support@viccom.se from the address linked to the account, if there is one.
  2. Put Delete account in the subject line.
  3. Tell us which game or app it's about and your display name.

We confirm within 7 days and delete the account within 30 days. If we can't tell that the account is yours, we'll ask you to confirm, for example by deleting it in the app.

Email support about deletion

What gets deleted

  • Your user ID and any link to Google or Apple sign-in
  • Email address and display name
  • Progress, garage, settings and saves in the cloud
  • Race results, records and leaderboard placings
  • Groups, alerts and push tokens (Psst)

What we keep

  • Backups of the database may contain your data for up to 30 days after deletion. Then it's gone.
  • Emails to support, including the deletion request, are kept for up to 24 months so we can show the request was handled.
  • Purchases made through Google Play or the App Store are handled by the store and aren't affected. Purchases made directly from VICCOM are kept for seven years under Swedish bookkeeping law.

Delete only some data

Want to keep the account but remove, say, your name from a leaderboard? Email support@viccom.se and tell us what you want removed.

More on how we handle personal data is in our privacy policy.
