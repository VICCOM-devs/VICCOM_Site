Wyrmwind

Race dragons through the storm.

Follow development

Wyrmwind is our dragon racing game. You ride a dragon through storm-torn skies, chain portals for speed and race other riders to the finish.

It's an early prototype. We're getting the flying to feel right before anything else, and we'll show more when there's something worth showing. It ships when it's done.
