Psst

One tap to alert your whole group.

Get notified

Psst sends an alert to everyone in your group with a single tap. Put the widget on your home screen and you don't even need to open the app.

It's built for teams and groups that need each other's attention right now: a coffee break, a customer at the desk, time to leave. One tap, everyone's phone buzzes.
