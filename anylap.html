AnyLap

A lap timer for RC cars that runs on your phone's camera.

Get notified

AnyLap turns your phone into a lap timer for RC cars. Set it up by the track, point the camera at the finish line, and it recognises each car as it crosses and times the lap.

No transponders and no timing loop. Lap times, stats and replays are saved on your phone, so you can compare runs and see if that setup change actually helped. No account, no ads, and nothing leaves the phone unless you share it.

  • PlatformAndroid first, iOS later
  • StatusIn development (v0.3)
  • PriceTo be announced
  • DeveloperVICCOM

How it works

  • Camera finish linePhotograph each car once. The phone watches the line and knows who crossed it.
  • Lap times as you driveSee each lap and your best lap while the session runs.
  • Stats and replaysCompare runs over time and replay each finish-line crossing. Everything stays on your phone.
