About the studio

Short

VICCOM is a one-person studio in Sweden making games and apps for mobile and desktop.

Long

VICCOM is a one-person studio in Sweden, founded in 2026 by Victor Immonen. The studio makes games and apps for mobile and desktop, focused on clear design, things worth coming back to and no pay to win. Its first game, Wobble Wheels, launches on Google Play in November 2026. The dragon racing game Wyrmwind, the RC lap timer AnyLap and the group alert app Psst are in development.

Games and apps

Wobble Wheels

A racing game where you draw your own wheels, then redraw them mid-race when the road changes. Spikes bite on ice, paddles get you through floods, small wheels fit low tunnels. Race friends live, chase world-record flags and climb ranked seasons.

Platform
Android on Google Play. Test build in your browser. iOS later.
Status
Closed test on Google Play
Launch
November 2026
Players

Price

Colours and type

The palette is called Frozen mist. Ember is used sparingly, as a signal.

  • Night#121311
  • Graphite#2A2B27
  • Moss#7C7D75
  • Mist#ADACA7
  • Ice#D9DADF
  • Frost#FCF8D8

Headings are set in Unbounded and body text in Familjen Grotesk, both free under the SIL Open Font License.
