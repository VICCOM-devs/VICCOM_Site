Wobble Wheels

Draw your wheels. Beat your friends.

Play the test build Follow the launch

The Google Play link goes here at launch. Until then, the browser version is the newest test build.

In Wobble Wheels the wheels are all that matter. You draw them yourself, with your finger, and redraw them any time during a race. The road keeps changing: glare ice, floods, low tunnels, mud and roots, speed bumps. Every surface wants a different wheel, and the best driver is the one who redraws first.

You race real players in live races, a weekly Sunday cup and ranked seasons from Bronze to Grandmaster. Every best run leaves a flag on the road, so you see exactly where the world record is.

Every car drives exactly the same. Bodies, paints, tyres and flags are looks only, and you earn them by playing.

How it works

  • Draw your wheelsDraw any shape on the pad. Spikes bite on ice, round wheels slide. The shape decides everything.
  • Redraw mid-raceSmall wheels for low tunnels, paddles for floods, cogs for mud and roots, big round ones for speed bumps.
  • Live online racesUp to five players in the same race, with boost and grip you choose when to use.
  • The Sunday cupOne week, one track, one winner. Every Sunday.
  • World-record flagsEvery best run leaves a flag on the road. Pass it and you're on record pace.
  • Ranked seasonsLeagues from Bronze to Grandmaster, with leaderboards and seasons.
  • 18 car bodiesHot dog, bathtub, shopping cart, rocket, skeleton car, ghost car, truck, Santa's sleigh and more. All drive the same.
  • No pay to winEarn the looks, not the stats. No car is faster than any other.
